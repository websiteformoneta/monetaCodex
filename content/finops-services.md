## The Shift

### Header

Cloud Resellers Are Expected to Do More

Cloud customers expect more than infrastructure. They expect guidance on how to manage cloud costs and improve cost efficiency.

For resellers, this creates both a challenge and an opportunity. Delivering Cloud FinOps requires consistent access to accurate billing data, clear cost visibility, and the ability to identify and implement savings opportunities across customer environments.

## The Problem

### Header

FinOps Tools Are Not Built for Resellers

Traditional Cloud FinOps tools are designed for companies managing their own cloud spend. They do not support resellers managing multiple customers, pricing models, and billing relationships.

They do not handle customer invoicing, reseller pricing, or margin management, leaving critical parts of reseller operations unsupported.

## The Solution

### Header

Cloud FinOps, Powered by Billing Infrastructure

moneta provides the billing, pricing, and margin visibility to deliver Cloud FinOps across every customer account. By aligning billing, discount programs, and cost data in a single system, resellers can move beyond one-time analysis and deliver structured, repeatable Cloud FinOps services.
