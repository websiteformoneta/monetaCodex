## What Moneta Is

### Header

A System Built for Cloud Resale Operations

Cloud resellers operate at the intersection of billing complexity, pricing strategy, and margin management. moneta brings these together in a single system purpose-built for AWS and Azure resale models.

Instead of relying on spreadsheets or adapting tools designed for end customers, moneta provides the infrastructure needed to manage billing, pricing, and profitability across every customer account.

## Platform Pillars

### Header

Core Platform Capabilities

#### Billing Infrastructure

Automate customer invoicing across complex cloud pricing models, customer agreements, and usage data. moneta ensures invoices are accurate, consistent, and fully aligned with your pricing strategy.

#### Cloud Discount Management

Identify and apply cloud discount programs across every customer account. moneta ensures savings are captured and correctly reflected in both cost and pricing.

#### Margin Intelligence

Gain visibility into profitability across every customer. moneta provides reliable margin reporting so you can understand performance, identify issues, and make informed pricing decisions.

#### Pricing Advantage

Use cost, discount, and margin visibility to create more competitive pricing. moneta enables resellers to win new business while maintaining healthy margins.

## How It Works

### Header

How moneta Works

1. Ingest Cloud Billing Data: moneta ingests AWS and Azure billing data, capturing usage, pricing, and discount information across all customer accounts.
2. Apply Pricing and Discounts: Customer-specific pricing models and discount programs are applied automatically, ensuring alignment between cost, pricing, and invoicing.
3. Generate Customer Invoices: moneta generates accurate, customer-ready invoice data based on your pricing strategy and cloud usage.
4. Track Margins and Performance: Gain visibility into margin performance across every customer, enabling continuous pricing optimization and profitability management.
