A System Built for Cloud Resale Operations

Cloud resellers operate at the intersection of billing complexity, pricing strategy, and margin management. moneta brings these together in a single system purpose-built for AWS and Azure resale models.

Instead of relying on spreadsheets or adapting tools designed for end customers, moneta provides the infrastructure needed to manage billing, pricing, and profitability across every customer account.
